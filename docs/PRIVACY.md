# Privacy and resource policy

- No gameplay or prompts leave the phone.
- No analytics, ads, account, or cloud-generation service exists.
- The one external asset is the official Qwen GGUF download.
- Deleting the model from the AI controls removes both completed and partial model files.
- The generator runs as a user-visible foreground service.
- The service yields for active gameplay, moderate heat, or low battery.
- The model is memory mapped and only one context exists.
- Saved catalog and player data use app-private SQLite storage.
